What If We Had Never Met?

Sometimes I wonder...

What if I had joined a different team that day?
Or what if you had started the match just a little earlier?

Maybe we'd have stayed complete strangers.

You would've just been another player in another random match.

I'd never know the girl who was busy eating biryani when we first met.

I'd never know what it feels like to be called “Kuposhit.”

I'd never become “Mr. Gentle.”

I'd never know that one random BGMI match would slowly turn into random calls, voice notes, doodles, inside jokes, and memories I'd always smile about.

I'd never know that someone I talked to for just a few minutes could one day become someone I'd care about this much.

I'd never know how someone could become such an important part of my life without me even realizing when it happened.

Maybe life would've gone on exactly the same...

But there would've been fewer smiles, fewer memories, and one very special person missing from it.

Out of billions of people in this world,
life somehow made our paths cross.

And no matter where those paths lead in the future,
I'll always be grateful that they crossed.

Because some people become memories...

You became one of my favourite chapters. ❤️

Thank you for being part of my story. ♡

A Letter For You 🤍

Well... I never thought that a random girl, who was busy eating biryani when we first met in BGMI WOW mode, would become someone I'd look forward to talking to every single day. 😭

I still smile whenever I think about how randomly we met. Out of all the matches I could've joined, I somehow ended up meeting you... and honestly, I'm really grateful I did.

Thank you for laughing with me through our absolutely unhinged conversations, for pointing out my mistakes instead of letting me stay wrong, for making even normal conversations memorable, for roasting me with "kuposhit", for calling me "Mr. Gentle" 😭, for your random voice notes, your doodles, your random calls, your laugh, and even for the moments when you make me overthink a little.

You've taught me that reassurance matters, that honesty matters, and that sometimes the smallest conversations become the biggest memories.

I don't know what the future looks like, but I do know one thing... meeting you was one of the happiest accidents that ever happened to me. And if I had the chance to join that BGMI match again, I'd still hope to find the same girl... probably eating biryani again. 🍗🤍

I'm glad that out of billions of people, life somehow made our paths cross. I'll always try to communicate honestly and keep learning from my mistakes.

Maybe one day our home will have your little library,a beautiful garden, and that swing you've always wanted. I hope every little dream of yours comesF true.❤
